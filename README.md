## Workflow

File an issue describing the question, proposed approach, and completion criteria. Store the experiment in `experiments/<issue_number>_<short_name>/`, beginning with a plan. Keep methods, small evidence tables, and conclusions in Git; keep large sequence databases in a documented cache with download manifests and checksums. Record unsuccessful searches and unresolved candidates as well as positive findings.

Changes may be committed and pushed directly to `main`.
